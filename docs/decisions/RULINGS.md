# Rulings on conflicts between records 0001-0009 (lead, 2026-11-14)

The nine records were written in parallel and disagree in places. These rulings are binding. Each record is edited to comply; where a record is named as owner of a topic, the other records refer to it and do not restate it.

## General

- **G1. Milestones live only in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.** Every record's "What lands when" section is reduced to two lists: "must exist from the first milestone because retrofitting is expensive" (each item with its reason) and "can be added later without redesign". No record assigns features to M1, first release or later.
- **G2. One owner per topic.** A number, name, layout or budget is stated once, in the owning record below. Other records cite it by decision id.
- **G3. No scheduled jobs.** Nothing runs unattended on a timer. "Nightly" checks become `check full`, run on demand.
- **G4. Reserved hooks.** A hook stays only if removing it would force a later change to the path state layout, the C ABI, or a kernel's signature. Everything else reserved "for later" is deleted from the records.

## Technical rulings

1. **Accumulation.** The path state carries no accumulated radiance. During a pass, contributions (including shadow-ray results that arrive after their path has ended) are added to a per-sample buffer in fp32; atomics are allowed there. At the end of the pass it is resolved into the film's fp64 moments with one writer per pixel and no atomics. 0008 owns the buffer and the resolve; 0002's `film_add` is renamed accordingly.
2. **Light-tracing splats** use the same normalised pixel filter footprint as camera samples (0008), not single-pixel deposits.
3. **Sampler.** 0003 owns it: definition, dimension map, and an "independent" mode for denoisers that need decorrelated frames. No seed value is special.
4. **Hero lane.** A random hero lane per path (0003 decision 6, with its bias counterexample) is binding everywhere, including media and geodesics.
5. **MIS.** The balance heuristic in the r_u / r_l form (0004) is used everywhere, including volumes.
6. **Names.** 0003's names win for packet operations and enums (`apply_frequency_shift`, `rewrite_wavelength`, `MAX_BOUNCE`, `GEODESIC_NUMERICAL`). 0002's names win for kernels and stages (`shade_light_path`, `generate_light`, `ShadeKind`). Cross-references use decision ids as they stand after this pass.
7. **Light-path wavelengths** are drawn from the same sensor wavelength table as camera paths and recomputed from the seed (0 bytes of state). Sampling proportional to an emitter's spectrum is not designed in; narrow-line emitters are a known noise case to measure.
8. **Medium stack** is 20 B per path (0006); the photon medium slot matches it. 0003 updates the budget.
9. **Path state** must total at most 256 B and shadow items at most 128 B with every field any record requires. If the sum does not fit, 0003 lists what is cut or moved to a side buffer and says which record loses the field.
10. **C ABI.** 0001's model stands: header `astroray.h`, handles `ar_context` / `ar_scene` / `ar_session`, every function returns `ar_status`, version is the header hash with exact match, no cross-version promise.
11. **Volume grids** cross the ABI as sparse 8³ leaves (0006).
12. **OIDN.** Our own build with renamed DLLs (0001), at one pinned version, produced once as a release asset and not part of the normal build.
13. **Kernel modules** are embedded in the release DLL; loose files only in development.
14. **Launch duration.** Target 20 ms per kernel launch for viewport responsiveness; hard cap 100 ms.
15. **CPU target** requires AVX2.
16. **One GPU compiler.** nvcc only; NVRTC is dropped (measured gain about 1 s per edit, not worth a second compile path). Revisit only if the measured edit loop exceeds 5 s.
17. **Register gate.** 0002's challenge is accepted and the spine is amended: the merge gate checks spills and stack in the shipping capped build against recorded allowances; the uncapped register count is a warning from `check full`.
18. **Geodesics.** `advance_geodesic` is a CUDA kernel that emits ray segments; segments go through the ordinary intersect kernel. No material, medium or VM code runs inside OptiX programs. Spine section 2 stands; 0006 is revised.
19. **VRAM.** One reserve rule and one budget table, in 0007.
20. **Texture decoding libraries** must be on 0001's dependency list or not used.
21. **First-frame gate.** At most 1.5x Cycles end to end as the gate, 1.0x as the goal, provisional until the owner answers; stated once in 0007.
22. **Mipmaps.** Texture filtering with mipmaps is part of the design; when it lands is the roadmap's business.
23. **Spine amendments accepted:** a marcher for media with no scattering and linear Mix for volumes (0006); a fixed upsampling convention per slot and the Add Shader policy (0005); the register gate (0002).

## Build-phase rulings

64. **Spike F part A, criterion (d).** The guide's "154-579 MB" is its Performance-mode figure at 1080p and 4K, not a range covering every preset. (d) is read as: NGX-reported memory for each preset and output size is within 5% of the guide's figure for that preset where the guide gives one, and every preset's figure is recorded for the VRAM ledger. Spike F measured Performance at 153.98 MB (1080p) and 596.7 MB (4K, +3%), so (d) passes; with (a)-(c) passing, part A passes and the DLSS-only items are built (0008 V12). DLAA at 4K needs about 1.45 GB, which the ledger's reserve rule (0007 d14) accounts for. The CUDA context must exist before NGX init, and preset-step recreate takes up to about 150 ms (input to 0007's hysteresis). Evidence: `docs/spikes/F-dlss/RESULTS.md`.

65. **Fault dumps out of process (0001 D2.5).** D2.5's filter "writes a minidump" is met by the filter starting `astroray_dump.exe`, shipped beside `astroray_core.dll`, which calls `MiniDumpWriteDump` on the faulting process with `ClientPointers = TRUE`; the filter waits with a bound and logs the outcome. Calling it in process deadlocked the fault tests in 10-20% of runs under concurrent load (it suspends every thread, then takes heap and loader locks; Microsoft's remarks say to call it from another process). Task 113.

66. **Instance identity (0007 d18).** `persistent_id` is relative to its instancer, so two instancers of one collection give equal ids. Object identity, and geometry identity when the evaluated data is not the datablock's shared copy, is (original object `session_uid`, parent original `session_uid`, `persistent_id`), following Cycles' `ObjectKey(parent, persistent_id, ...)` (`intern/cycles/blender/util.h`); 0007 V5 needs it. Task 080.

67. **Degenerate faces (0007 d9, V2).** A face whose triangulation has zero area (collinear or coincident corners) is skipped: it emits no triangles, is counted per mesh, and the count is logged once per mesh as a WARNING naming the mesh key. A face with area that ear clipping cannot triangulate (self-intersecting n-gon) falls back to a fan from its first corner, counted and warned the same way. Neither rejects the generation: Blender meshes routinely contain such faces and Cycles renders them; V2's "no degenerate triangles" holds because skipped faces emit none. Task 035.

68. **Representative motion step.** With S motion steps spaced evenly from shutter open to close (0007 d15) and ray time measured from the shutter centre (0003 D9), the step used for static data (triangulation, the instance centre transform, bounds seeds) is (S-1)/2 in integer division: the centre for odd S, the earlier middle step for even S. One helper defines it; mesh ingest (035) used S/2 and changes to it. Task 037.

69. **Generation numbers of latched views (0007 d9, d25).** Only `ar_edit_commit` allocates generation numbers, so a host learns an edit's outcome from `active_generation >= g`. A latch that carries view or settings changes but no newly finished commit keeps the active scene generation number; its frames are told apart by order and, when the image changed, by epoch. Display frames stay non-decreasing in generation, which is all `view_draw` relies on (d25). A per-session counter for views would break d25's single number space, and drawing a number from the scene at each latch would report a pending commit as active. Task 052.

70. **Device uploads and their failure (0007 d9, d11-d14).** Material and image arrays are built when their generation activates (059, 060) and the light table lazily (067, 068), so device copies are uploaded at a session's latch from the active host snapshot, once per scene snapshot and shared by the scene's sessions; each session pins the snapshot it latched, and a device table is freed when its last holder drops it (this is 0007 d9's retirement). An upload that fails at the latch keeps the session on its previously latched snapshot, sets `ar_progress.failed_generation` and raises one entry naming the table; the host generation stays active and the next latch retries. The image on screen therefore never shows a half-uploaded scene, which is what d9's rejection protects. Each session owns its CUDA device (own stream and globals) on the context's primary context and device index, with its own ledger (as CPU sessions, 052); the scene's device, which uploads scene tables, charges the scene's ledger. Tasks 102, 114.

71. **CLI overrides and bench fields (0001 D2.10, 0009 d22).** D2.10's sample rule is the CLI's default: without `--spp`, a final recording renders to its settings' count and a viewport recording to the CLI's. An explicit `--spp`, `--seed`, `--size` or `--device` overrides the recorded value for any recording, the CLI prints each override it applied, and an override the replay could not apply (no matching call in the recording) is an error. `--size` also sets the resolution to 100 % with no border and is refused when its aspect differs from the recorded view's. `astroray_cli bench` lines carry exactly d22's fields, and the tool that appends them to `bench/history` adds the append `date` that lint requires (metadata, not a measurement); `kernel_ms_per_sample` is the median pass wall time divided by the samples in that pass, which includes host work per pass until task 093's kernel timers replace it. Task 069.

72. **B-05 for shade_surface (owner decision, 0009 B-05).** NEE (task 074) puts shade_surface at about 4.6 s serial nvcc against B-05's 3.0 s. The owner chose an explicit raise for that one TU to 5.0 s over the D10 ladder's split (spike A: split surface kernels about 22 % slower at render time) or `__noinline__` (forbidden by 0002 D1): render throughput is unchanged and only that TU's rebuild is slower. Every other TU keeps 3.0 s. tools/kernelgate.py carries the override with this ruling's number; it is re-measured at each milestone, and growth past 5.0 s returns to the ladder. Task 074. Amended by owner decision 40: 7.0 s for M1b, re-measured at its close.

73. **The light alias test's uniform (0003 d23, 0004 D10).** The alias sampler needs its bin (32 bits of the light-pick dimension, offset 6 of the event block) and an independent uniform for the alias test; rescaling one uniform loses bits at large light counts (task 067). The per-event block has no free offset, so camera paths draw the alias-test uniform from per-path dimension 7 (DIM_CAM_UNUSED7, now DIM_CAM_ALIAS_Q), mixed with the event index through a bijective 32-bit mix (task 020's MixBits): uniform, and independent of every per-event dimension at each vertex. Light paths use their unused offset 6 the same way when they gain NEE (M5). Task 074.

74. **Requested passes the engine does not produce (0001 D2.11, 0008 d10).** A session's film follows the latched `ar_settings.passes` (task 117); a pass name outside what this build produces is not allocated, never fails the render, and raises the new code `PASS_UNSUPPORTED` (44, WARNING, SESSION scope, subject 0, detail = the pass name), one entry per name per latched settings change. `INPUT_IGNORED` is not reused: its subject is a material key. The C API reads a pass's list entry and its film under one render lock, because the film can now be rebuilt between two calls.

75. **Pass indices across a film rebuild (0007 decision 5, ruling 74).** Readback addresses passes by index, and a rebuilt film can shift every index after Combined, so a stale index could read another pass of the same size. Each session counts its pass-list revisions (a film whose channel list differs from the last published one); `ar_session_pass_count` records the revision it reported on the session handle, and `ar_session_pass_info` and `ar_session_read_pass` fail with `AR_ERR_INVALID_ARGUMENT` ("the pass list changed; call ar_session_pass_count again") when the film's revision differs. Callers enumerate with `pass_count` before reading; a caller that never enumerates is refused only after a rebuild. No ABI struct changes.

76. **Gate family sizes measured (0009 d15, Verification 14-15).** Task 078 injected a missing cosine in the shade stage, a wrong GGX VNDF Jacobian, a 1 % EON albedo error, a flipped front face and a hero lane fixed at lane 0 (in the sampler and in generate_camera), one at a time; `check gate` failed on every one at the current counts, and a test beyond m failed for both families. The gate suite's m = 2,000 and its sample counts are therefore measured, superseding d15's "PROVISIONAL" for the gate suite (the record's text is corrected when 0009 is next edited). analytic_scenes m = 256 stays in `[family_provisional]`, where lint pins it, until a task re-measures it. Two limits are recorded, not acted on: chi-squared cannot see an albedo scale (the energy tests carry it), and the per-pixel analytic z of 5.50 passes a 1 % albedo error on A1 and a4_sphere. Dropped transmission is measured when M2 adds the dielectric lobe. Result table: PR of task 078.

77. **NEE test modes (0004 D1 and D8 Verification rows).** With 0004's MIS as written, "NEE off" and "BSDF-only" are one estimator: dropping shadow items leaves emitter hits weighted by the BSDF pdf alone. The rows are read as three estimators that must agree: `on` (MIS), `off` (BSDF sampling only) and `light_only` (NEE weighted by the light pdf alone; emitter hits after a non-delta bounce add nothing; emission at the first vertex and after a delta bounce still counts). The gate-build hook `ASTRORAY_TEST_NEE=on|off|light_only` selects them. Task 106.

78. **JPEG decode parity (0007 d21, task 110).** PNG and TGA decode bit-exactly against Blender's `image.pixels`; JPEG is checked to at most 2 codes per channel, because JPEG decoders are not bit-specified (IDCT accuracy and chroma upsampling are implementation choices: stb_image versus Blender's libjpeg-turbo). Exact JPEG parity would need the engine to decode with libjpeg-turbo under Blender's settings; that is an owner question for later. 0005 V6's node-value bound (1e-4) is unchanged: 110 measures it against a reference render that cancels the spectral round trip and wavelength noise, not against raw film RGB.

79. **Order of the wavefront remedies (0002 D7, task 093).** D7 lists remedies (a) fixed grids and graph capture, (b) main-pool compaction, (c) a single-pass partition scan, in that order, as a plan made before any measurement. 093 measured B-1M and B-5M at 1080p: the partition is 61 % and 39 % of the pass, almost all in a scan that runs one block over every tile histogram; the material sort is 34.5 % of B-5M because its scatter tiles sum earlier tiles' columns; GPU idle between launches is 11.4 % on B-1M. The remedies are therefore taken in the order of measured cost: (c) first (task 118), the sort's scan (119), then (a) (120), each re-measured; (b) and spike C follow only if B-19 still fails after 120. B-20's comparison uses whole-pass wall time per 1080p sample until kernel timers exist (ruling 71); 093's kernel_ms_per_sample is per pixel and is not compared with Cycles.

80. **Remedy (a) as built, and when the material sort runs (0002 D7, tasks 120, 122).** 120 measured D7 remedy (a): fixed grids with CUDA graph capture were slower than stream-ordered transfers alone (empty launches on every step, a dearer round trip into a graph launch) and optixLaunch refuses stream capture, so remedy (a) is adopted as stream-ordered step transfers with one host wait per step; graphs are not used. With the sort at one warp per tile, 120 also measured B-5M (300 materials, one program) 9 % slower sorted than unsorted, with shading slower from scattered path-state accesses and no coherence gain: sorting pays only when materials run different programs. The sort therefore runs only when a segment holds at least kSortMinItems items and at least kSortMinMaterials distinct material programs (task 122); the key stays the material id. A locality-preserving key is written only if a multi-program scene measures the locality loss above the gain.

81. **The first context create and B-21 (0001 D2.8, 0009 B-21, tasks 094, 124).** D2.8 makes `ar_context_create` run the device checks synchronously so a missing or broken driver fails at create with AR_ERR_DEVICE; 124 measured the whole first-call cost of that check as the CUDA runtime's once-per-process initialization (cudart and nvcuda load, driver init: 13-16 ms standalone, 7.2-7.7 ms inside Blender), with later creates at 0.2 ms. D2.8 stands, and B-21 does not count that one-time initialization: it exempts only the first `ar_context_create` in a process, every later create and every other main-thread call stays within 5 ms, and a CPU context skips the CUDA probe altogether.

82. **V12's frame-per-pass rule and the B-34 publish cap (0007 V12, d50, 0009 B-34, task 130).** V12 asks that during an orbit every completed pass yields a frame for a newer view; B-34 caps publishing at 60 frames per second. For passes shorter than about 8 ms the two cannot both hold, and the cap wins: the orbit-gap bound is max(2 x pass p99, 1/60 s) + redraw interval + present + the host timer resolution (1 ms, which Blender requests), because the publish wait wakes up to one timer tick late. The single view-change bound is unchanged.

83. **V7a measured in the process (owner decision 35, 0007 d35, task 127).** V7a is the wall time from the timer callback that switches the region to Rendered shading to the first redraw of that region showing engine pixels, both timed inside Blender's process: a POST_PIXEL draw handler on the region timestamps every redraw (resolution: one redraw). Engine pixels are detected by reading a small patch of the region's framebuffer in that handler, with d35's rule (the first redraw close to the settled image whose successor is too). For an engine where that read is unusable (095: `read_color` in POST_PIXEL hung Blender under Cycles), the first redraw at or after that engine's own first displayable frame is used (Astroray: the present's first engine frame; Cycles: its first `Rendered N samples` update, on the same clock), and the two methods are shown to agree within one redraw on an engine where both work. V7a so defined leaves out only the compositor's presentation (at most one display refresh, alike for both engines) and keeps everything else d35 counts, sync and session and device creation included; harness, scenes, switches and the 1.5x gate are unchanged.

84. **The shadow pool's partition range (0002 D5, D6, D7; task 133).** 133 measured that main-pool compaction (D7 remedy (b)) cannot meet B-19's partition row at 1080p: the main pool fills once per pass and its whole partition share is 0.6-0.7 ms, while two thirds of every partition's items are dead shadow slots, because the shadow pool is partitioned over its full capacity at every step. D6 already says each pool is partitioned up to its high-water mark; the shadow pool now follows it. D5's free shadow slots become the shadow `TERMINATED` segment in index order followed by the fresh slots `[high_water, capacity)` in index order, the same rule the main pool's refill uses; the reservation stays deterministic and atomic-free, and the shadow high-water mark grows only by the fresh slots a step reserves. Main-pool compaction is not built; D7 (b) records 133's measurement.

85. **Partition marks that follow live slots (0002 D5, D6, D7; amends ruling 84; task 134).** 134 measured that, after ruling 84, the shadow pool's largest live slot falls from 1.68 M to under 1 K over a B-1M pass while its high-water mark stays at its peak, and that the main pool's largest live slot stays near its high-water mark (paths end scattered). Each pool's partition mark may therefore fall: `queue_build` reports per pool the end of the last 1024-slot tile holding a live slot (`StepTable::live_end`, an integer atomic max, deterministic), and the scheduler sets the next step's mark to that end plus the fresh slots the step reserves beyond it. The mark is tile-granular, not the exact slot, because exact variants pushed the count and scatter kernels over their 0002 D10 allowances; any mark between the largest live slot + 1 and its tile end gives the same partition. Free and fresh slots keep index order, so slot assignment, film, counters and the invalid total are bit-identical to marks that only grow (CPU tests force that with `keep_high_water`). Ruling 84's "grows only by the fresh slots a step reserves" and D5's matching sentence read "is set from the live end plus the fresh slots a step reserves". Main-pool compaction (D7 (b)) stays unbuilt. B-19's idle row is not a regression: no commit between 123 and 133 adds a gap (run-to-run spread 0.3 ms); its share rose because the pass shortened.

86. **Review tails may move (amends ruling 49).** Ruling 49 keeps each record's "Review and resolution" and "Reconciliation" sections in the record as history.
