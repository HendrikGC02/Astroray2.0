(a) The limit count needs no new field. (b) Bit-identity needs the stored state to be the estimator's state. Moving the origin and shortening the segment only at a requeue gives different float rounding from the uncapped loop (t_k = t_{k-1} + s against a fresh t from 0, and o + d t against (o + d t_c) + d t'), so capped and uncapped would differ in the last bits of positions and, rarely, in a reach-versus-collide comparison. Build it one of two ways and say which: (b1) the estimator takes a start distance t_min (pbrt SampleT_maj's tMin, media.h) and the requeued item keeps its origin and segment and stores the reached t, which needs somewhere to keep t across launches; or (b2) the loop rebases after every tentative collision in both runs (origin and remaining length updated per collision as the record's "moves its origin to the current point"), so a requeue is only a stop. (b2) changes 183's arithmetic: today's renders shift in the last bits, statistically unchanged, and the reached hit must still be reconstructed as before (shade_volume.h's REACHED branch builds the HitRecord from hit_t, prim and uv: check what shade_surface derives from hit_t).
