# 231 Machine lock: a hung holder stops itself, and short jobs go before whole tiers
Goal: a tool that hangs while holding the machine lock keeps every other worktree waiting until a waiter's 3,600 s `ASTRORAY_LOCK_TIMEOUT_S` (166's 8 s build waited 3,978 s behind a hung `check gate`, owner decision 42), and a one-file build waits behind every queued gate -> each lock-taking tool has a hold ceiling: past it, the holder prints `FAIL lock: held N s, over the <tool> ceiling` with its step, stops its own children and releases. A waiter names a hung holder within 15 min. A short job (`build.ps1`, `test.ps1` with one case) waiting for the lock goes before a waiting tier or canary.
